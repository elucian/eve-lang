## What you may do without asking

1. **Implement the language.** Anyone may write a compiler, interpreter, virtual machine, tool or library from the
   specification, under any license, and may call the language "EVE" to say which language it implements.
2. **State compatibility.** You may use these phrases, in plain text, to describe your own product:
   - "Built for EVE"
   - "EVE compatible"
   - "Runs EVE scripts"
   - "Based on the EVE language specification"
   They must be true, must not suggest that the Owner makes, endorses or supports your product, and must not be
   larger or more prominent than your own product name.
3. **Refer to the project.** You may name the project, the specification, the tutorial and the official virtual
   machine accurately (nominative use), for example in articles, books, courses, reviews, comparisons and
   package descriptions.
4. **Distribute the unmodified official software** under its license, with its name, licenses and notices intact.
5. **Name scripts, libraries and tools** for EVE in a descriptive way: "eve-yaml: a YAML library for EVE".

## What needs the "EVE Compliant" status

You may call an implementation an **"EVE Compiler"**, **"EVE Interpreter"**, **"EVE Virtual Machine"** or say that it
is **"EVE Compliant"** only if all these are true:

- it implements a stated version of the specification;
- it passes the official conformance tests of the level it claims (`test/`, levels 1 to 3), and it states that
  version and level in its documentation;
- it is not a modified copy of the official virtual machine (see below).

## What you may not do without written permission

1. **Publish a modified compiler, virtual machine or distribution under the primary EVE name.** A modified copy of
   the official software, or a fork, must be renamed and must not use "EVE" as its name or as the main word of its name
   (for example "EVE Pro", "EVE 2", "Super EVE", "EVE VM Community Edition"). You may say it is "based on the EVE
   language specification" or "a fork of the EVE virtual machine".
2. **Present an incompatible or extended dialect as EVE.** An implementation that changes the language, drops required
   features or adds extensions that change the meaning of valid EVE programs is not EVE and must say so.
3. **Use "EVE Compliant" or the official logo** on a product, a company, a domain name, a package name, a social
   media account or a certification mark in a way that suggests it is official, endorsed or certified by the Owner.
4. **Publish a modified specification under the EVE name.** You may adapt the specification for non-commercial use (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), but an adapted
   text must carry another title and state that it is not the official EVE specification.
5. **Register** the name, the logo or a confusingly similar mark as a trademark, a domain name or a company name.
6. **Alter the logo**: do not change its colors, proportions or shape, and do not combine it with other marks.

Written permission means an email or a signed letter from the Owner. Ask before you publish. Permission for one use
is not permission for another.

## Official logo

The official logo may be used only to link to the project or to refer to it (for example on a page that points to the
official tutorial). Use the files from the project, unmodified, with clear space around them. Do not use the logo as
the logo of your own product.

## Violations

If a use of the name or the logo breaks this policy, the Owner may ask you to stop. Please correct it in 30 days.
Rights granted by this policy end for a use that breaks it. This policy does not limit any right that the law gives you,
including fair, descriptive and nominative use.
